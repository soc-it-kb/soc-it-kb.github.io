IT-CL-SOP-02: Cloud Workload Baseline Governance

Defines how cloud workload baseline controls are governed, validated, evidenced, excepted, remediated and reported across AWS and Azure without creating duplicate policy sets.

Purpose
Govern baseline adoption and validation

This SOP defines how cloud workload baseline controls are approved, implemented, validated, evidenced, reported and excepted across AWS and Azure. It supports central control ownership while allowing each cloud team to implement through provider-native capabilities.

Roles
Accountability model
RoleResponsibility
Cyber SecurityOwns the control baseline, approves exceptions, validates evidence and reports residual risk.
AWS/Digital platform teamImplements AWS controls, supplies evidence, remediates gaps and maintains AWS-specific automation.
Azure/IT platform teamImplements Azure controls, supplies evidence, remediates gaps and maintains Azure-specific automation.
GRC/Risk ownerEnsures risk decisions, exceptions, residual risk and reporting are documented.
Business/workload ownerOwns business impact, risk acceptance and prioritisation of remediation for their workload.
Procedure
Baseline conformance assessment lifecycle
Step 1: Define scope

Confirm in-scope AWS accounts, Azure subscriptions, workloads, environments and data classifications.

Step 2: Issue assessment workbook

Provide the same baseline workbook to AWS and Azure teams, including evidence expectations and scoring rules.

Step 3: Complete team self-assessment

Cloud teams record status, implementation notes, evidence references, exceptions and remediation targets.

Step 4: Cyber evidence review

Cyber validates whether evidence demonstrates the control outcome, not just the existence of a tool.

Step 5: Gap classification

Classify each finding as design gap, implementation gap, evidence gap, monitoring gap, governance gap or risk acceptance gap.

Step 6: Treatment decision

Agree remediation, compensating control, accepted risk or time-bound deferral.

Step 7: Report and track

Record high-risk gaps in the IT risk register and report above-appetite risks through the GRC reporting route.

Step 8: Continuous validation

Move from point-in-time evidence to automated policy/compliance reporting wherever possible.

Conformance scoring
0–5 evidence-based scoring model
ScoreStatusMeaning
0Not implementedNo control exists or no evidence is available.
1Ad hocControl exists manually or inconsistently.
2Partially implementedControl applies to some workloads, accounts or subscriptions.
3ImplementedControl is consistently implemented for in-scope workloads.
4ManagedControl is implemented, monitored, evidenced and reviewed.
5OptimisedControl is automated, policy-as-code based and continuously reported.
